# aimhi-deal-desk
A governed commercial-real-estate underwriting Worker and public Deal Desk.

The public experience is organized around five client paths: underwriting
services, digital products, free investor tools and information, qualified
lender matching, and Aimhi Intelligence membership. It also includes a
purpose-routed intake, a free first-pass deal screen, and the existing
interactive monthly underwriting workbench with capital-stack analysis,
evidence status, risk register, and release checks.

Store and membership calls to action collect interest only. They do not claim
checkout, payment, subscription, protected fulfillment, or entitlement status
until those server-side provider flows are configured and verified.

Internal APIs persist immutable, versioned underwriting runs with D1
provenance, R2 model artifacts, audit events, and human approval gates.

See [IMPLEMENTATION.md](./IMPLEMENTATION.md) for bindings, deployment, security,
model-version, and API details.
